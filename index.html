Every action has a receipt

A member-service agent for a fictional credit union that completes core-banking actions (freeze a card, pay a loan, move money, change an address) and proves each one.

▶ Watch the tour (1:45, no sound)

Everything here is synthetic. Fernhollow Credit Union, its members and their accounts are invented, and the "core" is a small fake written for this demo; it does not model any vendor's core. Nothing here is a compliance claim.

It acts, it does not narrate

The model only asks for a tool. Code checks each call against a policy first; no matching rule means refused. Larger payments and every address change wait for a person, who approves the exact payload.

It proves what it did

Every write carries an idempotency key and lands as a row, and the runtime reads it back from the core before the member hears "done". The receipt shows what the agent said, what it called, which policy applied, who approved and what it cost. A pattern checker reads every reply before it is sent and holds any "done" the core cannot back.

Containment, measured honestly

59 of 60 scripted conversations pass. Whether each action happened is scored from the core's own rows, never from what the agent said. Containment means completed, not kept away from a person. Eval report.

A model change is a measured decision

Keep claude-opus-5: claude-sonnet-5 passed 54 of 60 against 59, more than the 2-case rerun noise. Benchmark.
